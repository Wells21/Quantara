== 3. Snippet 19.2 vs the paper
Paper eq.(12): sigma = sqrt(k beta / 2) - sqrt(k) alpha with k = 1/(4 log 2) (footnote 3: E[ln(H/L)] = sqrt(sigma^2/k) =
sqrt(2.77 sigma^2)), i.e. the squared-range constant k1 throughout. Substituting eq.(15) gives Snippet 19.2's form with sqrt(k1)
where the snippet has k2 = sqrt(8/pi) (the mean-range constant). Zero spread (beta = gamma = 2 k1 sigma^2): alpha = 0, paper
sigma = 1.0000, Snippet 19.2 = 1.0435 = sqrt(4 log 2) / sqrt(8/pi). A constant factor (no effect on tree splits).

== 4. Roll and the tick rule on mid quotes (s.19.3.1-19.3.2, exercise 19.2)
Roll c from tick-to-tick mid changes = 0.00-0.38 of the half quoted spread (mid has no bid-ask bounce; lag-1 autocorrelation of
mid changes -0.46 (BTCUSD) to +0.04 (XAUUSD) reflects quote updating, not trades). On bar closes: lag-1 autocorrelation
-0.021..+0.008. Zero mid changes (tick rule carries b_t-1): 0.0-5.7 % of ticks. The book's reason to use Roll (quotes absent
or unrepresentative, p.283) does not apply: we observe quotes; but our quotes are Dukascopy's, not the live broker's
(reference_broker_spread_decoupling), so an observed-spread feature would differ between research and live.

== 6. Book errata / checks (page images)
- p.287: alpha = p0 sqrt(sigma_u^2 / Sigma0) printed without the minus: the book's own alpha = -mu/(2 lambda) with mu = p0 and
  beta = 1/(2 lambda) gives alpha = -p0 sqrt(sigma_u^2 / Sigma0) (Kyle 1985: x = beta (v - p0)).
- p.292: E[V^B - V^S] middle line swaps the news terms: as printed alpha(1-delta)(eps-(mu+eps)) + alpha delta(mu+eps-eps)
  = alpha mu (2 delta - 1), not the stated alpha mu (1 - 2 delta); with the p.291 mixture (bad news, prob alpha delta, sells
  at mu + eps) the terms are alpha(1-delta)((mu+eps)-eps) + alpha delta(eps-(mu+eps)).
- Snippet 19.2 uses k2 where the paper uses sqrt(k1) (s.3 above).
- Snippets 19.1-19.2 do not run as printed: pd.stats.moments.* (removed from pandas in 0.23) and typeset en-dashes for minus.
- p.285 "Becker-Parkinson" vs Beckers [1983] (p.284 and references); p.293 heading "Distibution"; Carlin et al. cited [2007]
  on p.294, listed (2005) in the references; "Easley et al. [2012]" on pp.293-294 does not say 2012a or 2012b.
Checked correct: Roll sigma^2[dp] = 2c^2 + sigma_u^2, cov = -c^2, c and sigma_u^2 (p.283); Parkinson k1 = 4 log 2, k2 =
sqrt(8/pi) (p.284); C-S alpha and S (p.284 = paper eqs 15-16); Kyle x*, beta, lambda, E[pi] = (v-p0)^2/(4 lambda) (p.287);
PIN bid/ask, spread, delta = 1/2 case and the Poisson mixture (p.291); VPIN, V = alpha mu + 2 eps (p.292).
